Bears Are Real Here

The Smokies have one of the highest black bear densities in the eastern U.S. Encounters happen every day. Being prepared isn't paranoid — it's smart.

💨
Highly Effective

Bear spray has an excellent track record. Wildlife experts and park rangers consistently recommend it as one of the most effective deterrents in a close encounter.
